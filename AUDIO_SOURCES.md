### Rights review

VOA Learning English states that its Learning English texts, MP3s, photos, and videos are in the public domain and may be reprinted for educational and commercial purposes with credit to learningenglish.voanews.com.

Important limitation: VOA also states that third-party news-agency materials (for example, AP, Reuters, and AFP) remain copyrighted and must not be republished without permission. This project uses the VOA Learning English audio recording only. It does not republish the article's third-party photographs or other agency assets.

The audio player links directly to the original VOA audio file. The original article is linked for the complete transcript and context. The player depends on the continued availability of VOA's audio host.

### Editorial note

This is an original VOA Learning English educational broadcast with clear, learner-oriented narration, not a spontaneous airport conversation or a custom recording of every dialogue in our lesson. VOA describes its Learning English programs as being read about one-third slower than normal English speed. Use this as a clear first listening stage; add faster, spontaneous American speech as a separate, clearly labeled listening layer. Learners are directed to listen for selected airport expressions and compare them with the independently authored dialogues in American English Method.

## Future audio entries

Add a new entry for every external recording. Record the exact program page, direct media URL, creator/publisher, date, license or permission, attribution wording, and whether the asset is embedded, linked, or hosted locally. Do not assume that an audio file is reusable merely because it is publicly accessible or embedded in a broadcaster's page.
