# Audio Library — American English Method

This directory is the repository's central catalogue and home for original course audio.

## How we work

1. Prepare a complete lesson script and assign speakers before recording.
2. Generate all planned clips in one session.
3. Upload the MP3 files together into the matching lesson folder.
4. Keep filenames stable once they are referenced by a lesson.
5. Update this catalogue and the lesson player paths when new audio is added.

## Folder and filename convention

```text
audio/
  lesson-01-glass/
    01-nationwide-description.mp3
    02-dialogue-clarification.mp3
    03-southern-flavor.mp3
    audio-notes.md
```

Use lowercase English filenames, numbers for playback order, and descriptive names. Keep each clip as a separate MP3 when it is useful independently; combine clips into one continuous track when the lesson is designed as a single listening scene.

## Audio entry checklist

For every lesson folder, record:
- lesson title and lesson path;
- clip order and filename;
- speaker/voice used (for example, Brian or Emma);
- transcript or script version;
- generation date and source/service;
- rights or permitted-use basis;
- review status (not checked / checked / approved for publication).

## Important distinction

- **Original course recordings:** generated from our own scripts. Check the voice service's terms for publication and redistribution before publishing.
- **Third-party recordings:** keep their original source, attribution, and rights details in `AUDIO_SOURCES.md`. Do not copy a public audio URL into this library unless reuse is permitted.
