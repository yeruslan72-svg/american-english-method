## Recording script

On May tenth, seventeen seventy-five, representatives from twelve American colonies met in Philadelphia. Their meeting became known as the Second Continental Congress.

The battles of Lexington and Concord had already begun. At first, many delegates still hoped to make peace with Britain. But they also knew that the colonies needed to work together and prepare for war.

On June fourteenth, Congress established the Continental Army. The next day, it appointed George Washington, a respected military leader from Virginia, as commander-in-chief.

The Congress became the central government of the colonies during the Revolutionary War. It would later approve the Declaration of Independence on July fourth, seventeen seventy-six.

The Second Continental Congress helped turn separate colonies into a united political force.

Сражения при Лексингтоне и Конкорде уже начались. Поначалу многие делегаты всё ещё надеялись договориться о мире с Великобританией. Но они также понимали, что колониям необходимо действовать сообща и готовиться к войне.

14 июня Конгресс создал Континентальную армию. На следующий день он назначил Джорджа Вашингтона, уважаемого военного руководителя из Вирджинии, её главнокомандующим.

Второй Континентальный конгресс помог превратить отдельные колонии в единую политическую силу.

## American English in History

- **to take command** — принять командование.
- **to bring people together** — объединить людей.
- **to work together** — действовать сообща.
- **to make a decision** — принять решение.

In modern American English, *to take command* can mean to become responsible for leading a group or directing an operation.

## Editorial and historical notes

- The Second Continental Congress first convened in Philadelphia on May 10, 1775. Georgia did not initially send delegates; the script therefore says representatives from twelve colonies.
- Congress authorized the creation of the Continental Army on June 14, 1775, and appointed George Washington commander-in-chief on June 15.
- The Congress initially pursued both military preparation and possible reconciliation with Britain. The script summarizes this shift without suggesting that all delegates held the same views.
- The Second Continental Congress approved the Declaration of Independence on July 4, 1776.
