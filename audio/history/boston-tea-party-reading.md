# The Boston Tea Party — Listening Script

## Audio script

In December seventeen seventy-three, tensions between Britain and its American colonies were rising.

On the night of December sixteenth, protesters in Boston boarded ships carrying tea from the East India Company. They dumped the tea into Boston Harbor.

The protest became known as the Boston Tea Party. It became a powerful symbol of resistance to British authority.

But it was only one event in a much larger conflict. Fighting between Britain and the colonies began in April seventeen seventy-five.

## Russian translation

В декабре 1773 года напряжённость между Британией и её американскими колониями нарастала.

В ночь на 16 декабря протестующие в Бостоне поднялись на корабли, перевозившие чай Ост-Индской компании. Они выбросили чай в Бостонскую гавань.

Этот протест стал известен как Бостонское чаепитие. Он превратился в мощный символ сопротивления британской власти.

Но это было лишь одно событие в гораздо более масштабном конфликте. Вооружённое противостояние между Британией и колониями началось в апреле 1775 года.

## Listening task

1. When did the Boston Tea Party take place?
2. What did the protesters do with the tea?
3. What did the event become a symbol of?
4. When did fighting begin between Britain and the colonies?

**Speaking practice:** In your own words, explain why the Boston Tea Party became an important event in the history of the American Revolution.

## Editorial and historical notes

- The script describes the event as a protest and a symbol of resistance; it does not imply that the Boston Tea Party alone caused the American Revolution.
- The date and sequence are aligned with the AEM “The Birth of the United States” lesson.
